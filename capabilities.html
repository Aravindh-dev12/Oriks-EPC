Delivery lifecycle

Seven stages. One technical thread.

Requirement study — project, site, grid point, scope split and schedule
Engineering — layouts, interfaces, drawings and specifications
Procurement — equipment and material coordination
Civil & electrical execution — foundations, erection, cabling and installation
Protection & controls — relays, metering, interlocks and SCADA interfaces
Testing & utility interface — inspection, punch closure and energisation readiness
Handover & support — documentation, commissioning closure and O&M transition
