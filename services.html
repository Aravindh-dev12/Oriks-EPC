Services

Electrical EPC across the renewable-power chain.

The service structure is designed around how solar and wind projects actually reach the grid: plant electrical systems, pooling substations, evacuation infrastructure, utility interface, testing, commissioning and operations support.

01

Solar EPC & Electrical Works

Plant electrical infrastructure, collection systems, cabling, switchgear, earthing, interface works and grid-side coordination for solar projects.

02

Wind EPC & Electrical BoP

Electrical balance-of-plant support around wind projects, including collector systems, substation interfaces, evacuation and commissioning coordination.

03

Power Evacuation

Evacuation planning and execution from renewable generation to pooling station and onward to utility/TRANSCO grid connectivity.

04

Substations & Switchyards

Pooling substations, transformation, protection, metering, control, switchyard and associated civil/electrical scope.

05

Transmission Infrastructure

HT/EHT transmission lines, bay extensions, terminal interface, route coordination and grid-side execution support.

06

Protection, Metering & SCADA

Protection philosophy implementation, relay panels, metering interfaces, control systems, SCADA readiness and commissioning coordination.

07

Testing & Commissioning

Pre-commissioning checks, relay and equipment validation, documentation, energisation readiness, synchronisation support and handover.

08

Utility / EB Coordination

Support around project interface documentation, inspections, grid-connectivity requirements and utility-side coordination where applicable.

09

O&M Support

Preventive inspection, corrective support and electrical asset maintenance for substations, lines and renewable plant infrastructure.

Typical engagement

From scope definition to energisation.

Understand generation type, capacity, location and grid interconnection point
Define electrical scope, interfaces, evacuation route and substation requirements
Coordinate engineering, procurement and site execution
Complete testing, commissioning documentation and energisation readiness
Support handover and ongoing electrical asset care
