Services

Electrical infrastructure across the renewable-power chain.

ORIKS is positioned around the interfaces that help solar and wind projects reach the grid — plant electrical systems, pooling substations, power evacuation, transmission, protection, commissioning and O&M.

01

Solar & Wind EPC Support

Electrical and infrastructure support for renewable projects, including plant-side systems, collection networks, cabling, earthing, switchgear and site interfaces.

02

Power Evacuation

Planning and execution around the infrastructure required to move generated power from the plant to the approved grid interconnection point.

03

Substations & Switchyards

Pooling and grid-interface systems combining transformation, civil works, protection, metering, controls and associated electrical scope.

04

Transmission Infrastructure

HT/EHT evacuation lines, route coordination, erection and stringing interfaces, terminal arrangements and grid-side execution support.

05

Electrical BoP & Collection

Collection systems, cables, switchgear, earthing, auxiliary systems and plant electrical balance-of-plant interfaces.

06

Protection, Metering & SCADA

Protection, metering, control, interlock and SCADA-interface readiness as required by the approved project design and grid interface.

07

Testing & Commissioning

Pre-commissioning checks, functional verification, relay/equipment testing, documentation, energisation readiness and synchronisation support.

08

Utility / Grid Coordination

Support for project-interface documentation, inspections, metering/protection requirements and utility-side coordination where part of the agreed scope.

09

O&M Support

Preventive inspection, corrective support and electrical-asset maintenance for substations, evacuation lines and renewable plant infrastructure.

Typical engagement

From scope definition to energisation.

Understand generation type, location, capacity and grid interconnection point
Define electrical scope, interfaces, evacuation route and substation requirements
Coordinate engineering, procurement and site execution responsibilities
Complete inspection, testing, documentation and energisation readiness
Support handover and ongoing electrical asset care
