About ORIKS

Built around reliable energy infrastructure.

ORIKS Energy Care Pvt Ltd is presented as an EPC and electrical-infrastructure company serving the renewable-energy value chain, with emphasis on solar, wind, power evacuation, substations, transmission, grid connectivity and lifecycle support.

Our role

Helping renewable projects move power from plant to grid.

The strongest positioning for ORIKS is not simply “solar” or “wind.” It is the technical infrastructure that connects generation assets to the electrical network safely, compliantly and reliably.
