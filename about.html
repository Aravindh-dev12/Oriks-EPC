About ORIKS

Focused on the infrastructure that makes renewable power usable.

ORIKS Energy Care Pvt Ltd is positioned around renewable-energy EPC and electrical infrastructure — the systems that collect, transform, protect, evacuate and connect solar and wind power to the grid.

Our role

One connected view of renewable project delivery.

Solar and wind projects do not end at generation. Power must move through electrical collection systems, substations, protection, metering, transmission and commissioning before it can be safely delivered to the grid.

How we want clients to experience ORIKS

Clear, practical and accountable.

Every project conversation should begin with the real technical interface — site, capacity, voltage, interconnection point, scope split, approvals and schedule — rather than generic claims.

Operating principles

Clarity at every interface.

01

Engineering first

Understand the system, constraints, utility requirements and execution interfaces before defining the work package.

02

Site practicality

Translate drawings and specifications into buildable, maintainable electrical infrastructure on the ground.

03

Documented quality

Use inspection, testing, punch closure and handover records so completion is visible and verifiable.
